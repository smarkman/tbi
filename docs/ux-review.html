Website UX review and layout plan

How the current ShulCloud site works for visitors, what ShulCloud lets us change, and a recommended structure, homepage and page templates to mock up in Claude Design and then build in ShulCloud.

At a glance

The site has grown by adding a page for nearly everything, including calendar events and individual simchas. Visitors get very little structure, and the few pages that matter are hard to find. The fix is mostly subtraction and structure, which ShulCloud handles well, rather than a new theme.

How this was reviewed. The review is based on the full export of all 1,093 pages: settings, page HTML, links and images. It also uses the editing options visible in your ShulCloud admin. UX problems, in priority order

High

The homepage shows internal room holds as public events

The homepage events feed currently lists “HOLD FOR SET UP- Preschool Fall Festival Social Hall” next to “Preschool Music” and “Soccer Enrichment”. Facility bookings are flowing into the public calendar.

Fix: in the ShulCloud calendar, put room holds and setup blocks in a private calendar category. Point the homepage Events section only at public categories, such as Worship, Learning and Community.

High

No clear path for the people who visit a synagogue site

The homepage offers four tiles (Preschool, Religious School, Prayer, Become a Member). “Become a Member” goes to the generic /contact page, because there is no membership page. There are no service times, no address and no livestream link on the homepage. Each tile has a picture link and a “READ MORE” link pointing to the same place.

Fix: design for the five main visitors: prospective members, preschool and religious school parents, members looking for this week’s services, people marking a life event or loss, and donors. Each gets a direct route from the homepage and the menu (see Homepage).

High

Privacy and safety exposure

There are 131 public Bar and Bat Mitzvah pages with children’s names. Zoom links are posted on 7 public pages. About 25 staff and volunteer email addresses appear as plain text across the copy, which attracts spam.

Fix: set simcha pages to “Logged in Only” or retire them. Move livestream and Zoom links behind member login, or use a streaming page that doesn’t expose meeting IDs. Replace raw email addresses with role-based contact forms (ShulCloud Forms) or a single office address.

Medium

Too many pages, with no hierarchy

There are 1,093 pages, of which 729 have no body copy. There are 40+ separate board-member pages, “Clone of…” pages, and five High Holy Days pages, one per year (5782–5785). Web addresses are inconsistent: /learn/religious-school and /learning/religious-school are two different pages, and some addresses end in .html while others don’t.

Fix: cut down to about 60 public pages under 6 menu sections (see site map). Keep one page per topic that is reused every year, such as “High Holy Days” rather than “High Holidays 5785”, and set redirects from the old addresses.

Medium

Giving is scattered across many pages and services

There are separate pages for Funds, Tributes, Sponsorships, the Kol Nidre Appeal, Will Planning and several “pay” pages. Some send donors to an outside payment site (secure.acceptiva.com) and others to ShulCloud payments.

Fix: create one Give page with a clear choice: Donate, Honor or remember someone (tributes), Sponsor, Kol Nidre Appeal, Leave a legacy. Use ShulCloud’s own donation forms throughout so the experience and receipts are consistent.

Medium

Pages look different from each other because of pasted formatting

Copy has been pasted in from Word and Google Docs, bringing 4,348 inline style overrides and fonts such as Arial, Georgia, Verdana, Times New Roman, Trebuchet, Calibri and Lucida. 30 tables are used for layout, and 22 kept pages link to PDFs instead of putting the content on the page.

Fix: when rewriting, paste as plain text and use only the editor’s headings (H2/H3), lists and bold. Use ShulCloud’s column sections for layout instead of tables. Turn PDFs into pages, except for forms people actually print.

Medium

Accessibility gaps

Almost every image has no alt text, 369 links have no readable text, and some links just say “READ MORE” or “MORE INFO”. Screen-reader users hear “link, link, read more”.

Fix: give every image alt text, or mark it decorative. Write link text that says where the link goes (“Explore the preschool”). Use a single link per card.

Lower

Search results show weak titles and no summaries

Only 1 of 189 kept pages has a meta description, the summary Google shows under the link. 107 have no SEO title, so Google shows names like “Learn > Preschool > Summer Fun”.

Fix: while rewriting, fill in each page’s SEO title (“Summer Camp | TBI Preschool, Port Washington”) and a 1–2 sentence meta description. Both fields are already on every page’s settings screen.

Rules for page editors

  • Paste as plain text (Ctrl+Shift+V). Never paste formatted text from Word or Google Docs.
  • Use only H2 and H3 headings, lists, bold and links. Fonts and colors come from the theme.
  • Use column sections for layout, never tables. Use tables only for real tables, such as a dues chart.
  • Give every image alt text that describes it, or leave it empty if it’s purely decorative.
  • Make link text say where it goes. Never “click here” or “read more”.
  • Name pages and addresses simply: /learn/preschool/summer, no .html, no years.
  • Keep one page per topic that is reused every year: update it rather than cloning it, and delete clones.
  • Use the calendar for events, not new CMS pages. Room holds go in a private calendar category.
  • Fill in the SEO title and meta description whenever you publish a page.
  • No children’s names, meeting links or personal emails on public pages.

Rollout

Clean up (1–2 weeks)

Unpublish the ~900 empty, past-event and clone pages from the audit. Set simcha pages to members only. Move room holds out of the public calendar. Remove public Zoom links.

Mock up in Claude Design

Homepage plus the four templates, using the brief in docs/claude-design-brief.md. Review with clergy, staff and board.

Restructure the menu

Build the six landing pages, rebuild the navigation, and set redirects from old addresses.

Rewrite and rebuild pages

Work from audit/tbi-site-copy.md, page by page, in the new templates: Join, Give, Preschool and Services first.

Final pass

Alt text, link text, SEO titles and descriptions, and a phone check of every landing page.
